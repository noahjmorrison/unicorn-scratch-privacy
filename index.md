# Unicorn Scratch Art Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Unicorn Scratch Art is a scratch-and-reveal art game made for children. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

## What the app collects

The app has no accounts, sign-in, chat, or analytics of its own. Google advertising and purchase services process information as described below.

The app saves the following in its private storage:

- which unicorn friends have been found and how many times,
- the picture currently being scratched and its progress,
- story chapter progress,
- artwork made in the decorating studio,
- settings such as brush size, sound, voice, and Easy mode.

Android may back up and restore app data according to your device and account settings. Uninstalling removes the local app data but may not remove existing Android backups. Scratch marks use temporary cache storage and may be removed by Android when it needs space; collected friends, story bookmarks and saved artwork use durable app storage.

## Your own photos

My Photos lets you choose one image or a folder through Android's file picker. A chosen provider may include cloud storage. The app reads the selected image, or images directly inside the selected folder, for scratching. It remembers the selection and retains Android read access so the picture can be reopened after restarting the device. Choosing a new picture or folder changes the active selection; it does not automatically revoke older retained access that saved pictures may still need.

The app does not itself upload your photos. Android and the chosen provider may retrieve, transfer or back up files according to their settings and policies. Saved artwork made from a photo keeps a private copy inside the app so it can remain available if the original photo is moved. You can manage or remove the app's access and stored data using Android's available app controls or by uninstalling the app. Removing local app data does not necessarily remove provider files or existing Android backups.

## Advertising

The app shows advertising through Google AdMob. The app configures Google AdMob for child treatment and the G (general audiences) ad content rating. These settings are intended to restrict advertising for children and prevent personalised advertising. Google's handling of ad requests is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [AdMob policies for families](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431).

To show ads, Google's ad software sends Google the device's IP address (which gives an approximate location), information about how the app and its ads are used, diagnostic information, and device or app-related identifiers used by Google's advertising software. The app does not use the Android advertising ID.

## Purchases

The app offers one optional purchase, Remove ads, which turns off all advertising. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play Billing, so the app never sees or stores payment details. Google Play keeps a record of the purchase so it can be restored on the same Google account. See the [Google Payments Privacy Notice](https://payments.google.com/legaldocument?family=0.privacynotice).

## Internet access

The app uses internet access for advertisements and to check whether the Remove ads purchase is owned. Android and a chosen file provider may also use the internet to retrieve selected photos or back up data according to their settings. When recorded narration is unavailable or fails, the app sends story or guidance text to your device's speech service. Whether that service uses the internet depends on its engine and settings. The app includes pictures, story text and recorded narration that can be used offline. If a recording is unavailable, the app may use your device's text-to-speech service. That service's availability, internet use and information handling depend on your installed speech engine and device settings.

## Children's privacy

The app is designed for children. It does not ask children for names, contact details, or accounts. Private app data and Android backups are described above. Data sent to Google to show ads is handled under the Google Privacy Policy. If you have questions about your child's information, contact the developer at the address below.
